# Documentation

Short notes on each problem found while making the game run, and what was done about it. Each one gives
the symptom, the cause, the fix and how to turn the fix off, so that you can check it on your own machine.

| Note | Status |
|---|---|
| [The fixes ported from Guitar Hero 5](fixes-from-gh5.md) | In testing; frame rates above 240 (300, 360, 480, Unlimited with V-Sync off) played by Gustavo, 2026-11-18 |
| [Ambient occlusion and texture detail](native-x-renderer-ghwor.md#ambient-occlusion-and-texture-detail-nativexrenderer-d178-d179-2026-11-18) | Played: NativeXRenderer's XeGTAO (D178) in place of the game's own, the camera from the view's draws; the 3D view's mip bias (D179) |
| [Speakers and the game's Dolby Digital](speakers-and-dolby.md) | Played: the game told its speakers (Dolby Digital only with 5.1), its timing kept for every choice |
| [NativeXRenderer in Guitar Hero: Warriors of Rock](native-x-renderer-ghwor.md) | In testing, the default renderer: SMAA and FXAA on the scene's copy, and DLAA, DLSS and FSR 3.1 once a pair of frames with the split render (NativeXRenderer's D159); two launcher rows for them, off by default and in testing: the screen effects before the upscaler and DLSS one frame late (D164, DLSS's upscaling modes only, not DLAA) |
| [Downloadable content](dlc.md) | Working: the player's DLC folder, read in place (0.1.1-beta.2); Guitar Hero 5's Megadeth Track Pack adds its songs to Quickplay+ |
| [Stutters the first time something is shown](compiling-shaders.md) | Fixed: a pipeline list ships with the game; the launcher compiles it for your graphics card at the first Play ("Compiling shaders"; NativeXRenderer) |
| [The notes ahead of the music when a song follows another](song-sync.md) | Fixed on branch `fix/song-sync`, played: a song after a cinematic (the Rush chapter's parts) no longer starts its clock past the music's start with an audio calibration; the music starts within a frame of its time above 60 FPS |
| [The notes drifting from the music during a song](song-clock.md) | Fixed on branch `fix/song-sync`, measured: with V-Sync off the notes gained up to 73 ms in a song (the SDK's guest clock counted time twice between threads, and the game's own sum of the vertical blanks), they stepped at each hitch, and a late audio device callback left the music 10 ms late for good; the song's clock from the blanks' own times and the device's gaps made up |
| [The game ending in a song: the lighting job on the wrong scene](lighting-job.md) | Fixed (`ghwor_lighting_job_wait`, on), measured: the render thread no longer moves to the next scene while the lighting job it queued has not run; a late job read the next scene (a wrong light volume for a frame, or a crash on a busy CPU) |
| [Crackling in songs on slower PCs](audio-crackling.md) | Fixed in the SDK (change 82): the audio callback's spacing counted from its start, so a slower CPU keeps up |
| [The fixes from GH Metallica of 2026-11-11 to 13](fixes-from-gh-metallica.md) | Built, not tried in the game yet: the game's debug menu, the logos ended once the game has loaded and the intro movie left out, drums on five or four lanes (five lanes fixed 2026-11-15: the game wanted a Guitar Hero kit's sign, checked with the keyboard in Party Play), Settings > Audio and the graphics card; the tap notes' trail not ported (this game draws no trail); online play since in [its own note](online-play.md) |
| [The mouse cursor](mouse-cursor.md) | Hidden over the game after 2 s without moving it (SDK change 103) |
| [Xbox One guitars (PDP Riffmaster)](xbox-one-guitars.md) | Built, a player's test to come: read through Microsoft GameInput (SDK change 110), the tilt as an Xbox 360 guitar's, the launcher offering GameInput when it is missing (3.23) |
| [Online play](online-play.md) | In testing: two copies on one PC and two PCs through Steam played songs together, joined by invites (SDK changes 106-109 and 111: the party's key, socket numbers, no port without online play, the friends list kept up to date, an invite's join spent) |
| [The venue at the full frame rate](scene-every-frame.md) | Built, off by default: the engine's split of the 3D scene over two frames turned off (the venue and the band drawn in every frame, about twice the 3D work); checked in Party Play on both renderers |
| [D3D's fence stamps and the page watches](fence-stamps.md) | The stamps written where the watches do not see them; 240 FPS held in a busy song with the venue every frame; the render objects' frame and the textures' fence too (Phase 3) |
| [A controller plays as in Guitar Hero III](controller-gh3-layout.md) | Played with a DualSense: the game's own GH III controller code, chosen by hooks (SDK change 112, the default; a switch in the launcher), extended sustains, and its own buttons in a song (SDK change 113: the launcher's "In a song" and "Navigation") |
| [Guitar Hero World Tour's Star Power sound](star-power-reverb.md) | Played, off by default: Star Power puts the player's instrument through World Tour's echo, reverb and high pass, and the echo sounded right in songs (2026-11-15). Two settings in `ghwor.toml` raise it |
| [Uneven frame times after a hitch](uneven-frames-after-a-hitch.md) | Fixed in NativeXRenderer (D172), a player's test to come: with V-Sync and the record thread, a frame left waiting for the display after a hitch is taken away by not showing one frame |
| [The Mods tab: the note highway, its pictures, the crowd and two sounds](mods.md) | The highway and the crowd played: its scale with one player, its length (notes seen earlier at the same speed, the console's perspective continued) and perspective (the notes at the console's speed with it: checked with the bot), a dark style, the crowd hidden. The whammy's sound off and the instrument's volume on a miss (0-100 %): played. The highway's pictures (a gallery, one or a random one at each song, bright ones darkened; NativeXRenderer's D177), a see-through highway, and custom textures from a folder (D176): built, a test to come. The launcher's Mods tab (3.30.0) with a preview from the game's own textures |
